# Scan final recalibration detector comparison

This is the finalized record of a six-configuration YOLO-World v2 comparison
on one controlled 360-degree source scene. The immutable source and physical
trial RunBundle IDs are in [runs.yaml](runs.yaml); the compact conclusions and
visibility-annotation results are in [results.md](results.md).

The full RunBundles, source media, completed `latest_task` comparison replay,
and HTML report remain ignored under `runs/`. This study does not claim mAP or
general detector accuracy; it records scene-presence/visibility behavior for
the bounded scene.
